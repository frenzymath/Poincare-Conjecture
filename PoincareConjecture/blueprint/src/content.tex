\chapter*{The Poincare Formalization}

This blueprint explains the proof implemented in the PoincareLib library.
Its eighteen chapters follow the geometric argument through Ricci flow,
controlled surgery, finite extinction, and connected-sum reconstruction,
then develop the compatible smoothing needed for the topological theorem.

The Lean formalization has passed a full build and Comparator verification.
This exposition is a draft snapshot of Archon Horizon's workspace at
\texttt{fbdf7e1493ed}. Seventeen chapters have passed Horizon's source and
readability review; the smoothing chapter and whole-book publication review
remain pending. These editorial reviews are separate from proof verification.

Lean links identify the declarations attached to individual statements.
The website's progress measures those links, not the completion of the
entire formalization. Some explanatory statements have no attached Lean
declaration, and three historical surgery adapters are discussed only to
explain the earlier development. The reference projects are reading material
and have no formalization progress counters.
