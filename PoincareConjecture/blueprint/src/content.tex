\chapter*{About This Blueprint}

This is a custom blueprint for the Poincare conjecture. It reorganizes the
mathematical arguments according to their logical dependencies, grouping
statements by their role in the proof and retaining the definitions and results
needed for the final argument. Standard prerequisites outside the main route
are recorded as precise, source-linked interfaces.

The proof is organized in six stages. Chapter 1 establishes the comparison
tools and the topology of necks and caps. Chapter 2 develops reduced geometry,
local estimates, compactness, and noncollapsing. Chapter 3 classifies
kappa-solutions and uses canonical neighborhoods to continue surgery for all
time. Chapter 4 develops the curve-shrinking estimates used to measure surviving
topology. Chapter 5 turns those estimates into finite extinction. Chapter 6
reconstructs the initial manifold across the surgeries and completes the
topological argument. The dependency graph records the logical flow between
these stages and ends at the Poincare conjecture as its unique terminal
statement.
