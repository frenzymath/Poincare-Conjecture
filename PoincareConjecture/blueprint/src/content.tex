\chapter*{About This Blueprint}

This blueprint records a formalization-oriented proof of the Poincare
conjecture along the Morgan--Tian route.  Its chapters are ordered by logical
role rather than by source order.  Each retained result is stated with the
hypotheses used by its consumers, and each nonlocal input is tied to an exact
source coordinate.  Kleiner--Lott is used only for source-exact surgery
interfaces that repair or disambiguate the corresponding Morgan--Tian
contracts.

The proof has six stages.  Chapter 1 establishes forward-difference comparison,
the local surgery metric, and the Appendix classification of regions covered
by canonical necks and caps.  Chapter 2 develops reduced geometry,
noncollapsing, canonical neighborhoods, and the cutoff conditions.  Chapter 3
classifies kappa-solutions and continues controlled surgery for all time.
Chapter 4 supplies the corrected curve-shrinking estimates.  Chapter 5 turns
two-sphere and loop-space widths into finite extinction.  Chapter 6 tracks
connected-sum topology across every surgery and specializes the extinct-flow
classification to a closed simply connected three-manifold.

This evolving revision is a mathematically closed, source-backed candidate
Blueprint pending human expert review.  The Hempel sphere/projective-plane,
Plateau--Morrey, Douglas--Hildebrandt, and compact-parametric parabolic-flow
results are explicit imported contracts: each states the exact hypotheses
consumed downstream and cites a registered Morgan--Tian, White, Topping, or
Perelman location.  Their classical source proofs remain within the human
review boundary, not hidden in the terminal theorem.  The live semantic graph
has 279 declarations and 854 direct prerequisite edges; these counts are
consequences of the mathematical decomposition, not completion targets.  Every
live statement remains marked \verb|\notready| because no claim of Lean
formalization or independent expert approval is made.
