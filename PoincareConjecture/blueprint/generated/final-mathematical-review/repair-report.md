# Repair Ledger

This ledger records repairs made to the live Blueprint after the final
mathematical review.  The original review files remain an immutable audit of
the pre-repair snapshot.

## Direct statement repairs

- Neck distance estimates are explicitly intrinsic, overlap conclusions require
  middle-region intersection, and the external-point comparison includes the
  neck scale and a no-exit geodesic hypothesis.
- Reduced-volume transfer is a push-forward measure contract with an explicit
  measurable endpoint map, absolute continuity, support, and terminal density
  bound.  Downstream noncollapsing and kappa-solution uses now state this
  endpoint-transfer assumption.
- Surgery maps assert $(1+\eta)$ metric comparison and Lipschitz control rather
  than an exact isometry; global gluing is conditional on an explicit collar
  interpolation estimate.
- The minimal-sphere variation lemma is restricted to three-dimensional Ricci
  flow, and the kappa-solution definition is dimension-qualified.
- Curve-flow well-posedness is the verified one-loop contract.  Compact-family
  existence, uniqueness, continuation, and uniform derivative estimates are
  explicit hypotheses of the family interface; the 2015 correction is recorded
  as a source.
- Annular and least-disk statements no longer claim unproved minimizer
  attainment.  Annular variation, small-annulus transfer, and loop-family
  deformation expose the required attainment, regularity, and quantitative
  estimates as hypotheses.
- Surgery-time finiteness, component fundamental-group persistence, essential
  sphere finiteness, free-product obstruction, projective-plane exclusion, and
  one-surgery reconstruction now state their missing finite-complexity,
  topology, or saturation assumptions.  The first two elementary consequences
  have direct local proofs.
- Published MT coordinates replace the stale Chapter 1/2 and Chapter 18/19
  transcription ordinals.  Hatcher Basic 3-Manifold Topology uses the separate
  `Hatcher3M` namespace.
- The all-time flow theorem explicitly includes the one-surgery topology output,
  restoring the canonical route obligation.
