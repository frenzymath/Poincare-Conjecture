\chapter*{About This Blueprint}

This project follows Bruce Kleiner and John Lott, \emph{Notes on Perelman's
Papers} \cite{KleinerLottwebsite}. A PDF version is available
\href{https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0605667}{here}.

Kleiner and Lott give a detailed account of Perelman's Ricci-flow proof of
geometrization. The notes develop entropy and reduced-volume monotonicity,
noncollapsing, pseudolocality, canonical neighborhoods, $\kappa$-solutions,
Ricci flow with surgery, and the long-time thick-thin analysis. In the
Poincar\'e project, this source records the main Ricci-flow route from
Perelman's estimates to the Poincar\'e and Geometrization conjectures.

The blueprint is a concise, independently worded mathematical distillation,
not a transcription of the notes. It retains the statements, conventions,
cross-references, and proof ideas needed for formalization. \textbf{We
recommend reading the blueprint alongside the notes.} The \emph{dcref} tag
identifies the corresponding section, theorem, definition, or other numbered
item in Kleiner--Lott and is the intended guide for comparison with the
source.

The blueprint covers the introduction, Ricci flow without surgery, Ricci flow
with surgery, and the analytic and topological appendices. Source section
numbers are kept in \emph{dcref} metadata rather than rendered as chapter or
section titles.
