\chapter*{About This Blueprint}

This project follows Huai-Dong Cao and Xi-Ping Zhu,
\emph{Hamilton-Perelman's Proof of the Poincar\'e Conjecture and the
Geometrization Conjecture}, published in the \emph{Asian Journal of
Mathematics} in 2006 \cite{CaoZhu2006}. The source PDF is available on
\href{https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0612069}{arXiv}.

The source develops Hamilton--Perelman's Ricci-flow program: evolution and
maximum-principle estimates, reduced volume and noncollapsing, singularity
models, surgery, long-time geometry, and geometrization. These results supply
the Ricci-flow analysis used to formalize the Poincar\'e conjecture and
geometrization.

The blueprint is a concise, independently worded distillation, not a verbatim
copy of the source. It retains statements and proof ideas needed for
formalization. \textbf{We recommend reading the blueprint alongside the
source PDF.} The \emph{dcref} tag records the corresponding theorem,
definition, or other source number in Cao--Zhu's work.

The blueprint covers all eight chapters: Ricci-flow equations, maximum
principles, reduced volume, singularity formation, long-time behavior,
ancient $\kappa$-solutions, Ricci flow with surgery, and geometrization.
