\chapter*{About This Blueprint}

This project follows Bennett Chow and Dan Knopf, \emph{The Ricci Flow: An
Introduction}, Mathematical Surveys and Monographs 110 (American Mathematical
Society, 2004) \cite{chowKnopf2004ricci}.

The book develops the analytic and geometric foundations of Ricci flow used in
the Poincar\'e project: homogeneous and self-similar solutions, short-time
existence, maximum principles, surface flows, positive Ricci curvature in
dimension three, derivative estimates, singularity models, and Type I
singularities. These results provide Ricci-flow background rather than a
standalone proof of the Poincar\'e conjecture.

The blueprint is a concise, independently worded mathematical distillation,
not a transcription of the book. It retains the statements, conventions,
cross-references, and proof ideas needed for formalization. \textbf{We
recommend reading the blueprint alongside the book.} The \emph{dcref} tag
identifies the corresponding numbered item in the source.

The blueprint covers all nine chapters and both mathematical appendices:
Ricci calculus and comparison geometry.
