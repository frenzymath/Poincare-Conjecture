\chapter*{About This Blueprint}

This project follows Peter Topping, \emph{Lectures on the Ricci Flow},
published by Cambridge University Press in 2006 \cite{topping2006ricci}.

The book develops the Ricci-flow material used throughout the Poincare project:
Riemannian and parabolic preliminaries, curvature evolution, maximum
principles, short-time existence, compactness, Perelman entropy, and
three-dimensional curvature pinching. These results are background for the
formalization of the Poincare conjecture rather than a standalone proof of it.

The blueprint is a concise, independently worded distillation, not a verbatim
copy of the book; it retains statements and proof ideas needed for
formalization. \textbf{We recommend you to read the blueprint alongside the
book}. The \emph{dcref} tag records the corresponding numbered source location
in the form \texttt{chN:section.item}.

The blueprint covers Chapters 1--10 and the connected-sum appendix: the Ricci
flow equation and special solutions, geometric variation formulae, maximum
principles, parabolic existence theory, gradient flow, compactness, Perelman
entropy, curvature pinching, and positive Ricci curvature in dimension three.
