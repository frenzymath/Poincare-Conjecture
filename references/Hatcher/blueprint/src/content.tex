\chapter*{About This Blueprint}

This project follows Allen Hatcher, \emph{Algebraic Topology} (Cambridge
University Press, 2002) \cite{Hatcher2002}.
A PDF version is available
\href{https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/AT/AT.pdf}{here}.

Hatcher develops the foundational tools of algebraic topology through geometric
constructions and computable invariants.  In the broader Poincar\'e conjecture
project, this material supplies the language of homotopy and cell complexes,
the theory of fundamental groups and covering spaces, and the basic machinery
of homology.  These tools organize many of the topological constructions and
obstructions that surround the conjecture, although this blueprint is a
reference development rather than a proof of the conjecture itself.

The blueprint is a concise, independently worded mathematical distillation,
not a transcription of the source.  We recommend reading it alongside
Hatcher's book, which provides the geometric motivation, examples, figures,
and fuller proofs.  The \emph{dcref} tag on each item records the corresponding
location in the source so that the distilled statement can be checked against
its original context.

The current blueprint covers Chapter 0 and Chapters 1--2: underlying geometric
notions, the fundamental group and covering spaces, and homology.  Later
chapters of the book have not yet been transcribed.
