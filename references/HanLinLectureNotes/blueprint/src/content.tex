\chapter*{About This Blueprint}

This project follows Qing Han and Fang-Hua Lin, \emph{Lecture Notes on
Elliptic Differential Equations}. The source PDF is available from the
\href{http://home.ustc.edu.cn/\%7Eanprin/EllipticEq-Han-Lin.pdf}{authors' archive}.

The notes provide elliptic PDE foundations used throughout geometric analysis:
mean-value and maximum principles, boundary and interior estimates, weak
solution methods, regularity theory, and viscosity solutions. These tools
support the analytic arguments surrounding Ricci flow and the Poincare
conjecture; they are background for that formalization rather than a standalone
proof of the conjecture.

This blueprint is a concise, independently worded distillation, not a verbatim
copy of the notes. It retains the definitions, statements, estimates, and proof
ideas needed for formalization and is intended to be read alongside the source.
The \texttt{\dcref} tag records the corresponding definition, theorem,
lemma, corollary, example, or remark in the notes.

The blueprint covers all five chapters: harmonic functions; Hopf and
Alexandroff maximum principles and their applications; weak solutions,
Campanato and De Giorgi methods, and Harnack estimates; nonlinear regularity;
and viscosity-solution approaches to Schauder and $W^{2,p}$ estimates.
