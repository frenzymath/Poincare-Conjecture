\chapter*{About This Blueprint}

This project follows William P. Thurston, \emph{The Geometry and Topology of
Three-Manifolds}, electronic version 1.1 (March 2002), the MSRI/SLMath edition
of his Princeton lecture notes, originally delivered in 1978--1980
\cite{thurstonGT3M}. The edition is available from
\href{https://library.slmath.org/nonmsri/gt3m/}{SLMath}.

Thurston develops the geometric and topological language behind the
three-manifold geometrization program: presentations of three-manifolds,
elliptic and hyperbolic models, geometric structures and their deformation,
hyperbolic Dehn surgery, rigidity and volume, Kleinian groups, algebraic
convergence, deformations at infinity, and orbifolds. In the wider
Poincare/geometrization formalization, these chapters supply the bridge from
low-dimensional topology to hyperbolic geometry; this blueprint is a reference
development, not by itself a proof of the geometrization or Poincare theorem.

The blueprint is a concise, independently worded mathematical distillation,
not a transcription of the notes. It retains definitions, hypotheses,
statements, formulas, exceptional cases, and proof ideas that are useful for
formalization, while omitting source exposition, motivation, figures, and
repetition. We recommend reading it alongside the source. The \emph{source}
metadata on each declaration records the audited original printed page(s), and
\emph{dcref}, when present, records the corresponding numbered item in the
notes; together they provide the comparison trail without reproducing the
source text.

The current audited coverage consists of Chapters 1--9, 11, and 13. Chapters
10 and 12 are absent from the electronic edition and therefore are not gaps in
this blueprint; the index and errata are likewise kept only in the reference
archive. Every included chapter has been independently reviewed for
mathematical fidelity and for source-prose overlap before integration.
