\chapter*{About This Blueprint}

This project follows Lawrence C. Evans, \emph{Partial Differential Equations},
second edition, Graduate Studies in Mathematics 19 (American Mathematical
Society, 2010) \cite{Evans2010}.
A PDF version is available
\href{https://math24.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/02/partial-differential-equations-by-evans.pdf}{here}.

Evans develops the analytic foundations of partial differential equations:
first- and second-order equations, representation formulas, Sobolev spaces,
elliptic and evolution equations, the calculus of variations,
Hamilton--Jacobi equations, and systems of conservation laws. These tools
support the Poincare project through the analysis of geometric evolution
equations and Ricci flow; this blueprint is background for that formalization,
not a standalone proof of the Poincare conjecture.

The blueprint is a concise, independently worded mathematical distillation,
not a transcription of the book. It retains the statements, conventions, and
proof ideas needed for formalization. \textbf{We recommend reading the
blueprint alongside the book.} The \emph{dcref} tag identifies the
corresponding theorem, definition, or other numbered item in Evans's text and
is the intended guide for comparison with the source.

The blueprint covers all eleven chapters and all five appendices: the main
linear and nonlinear PDE theories, Sobolev and functional-analytic tools,
variational and nonvariational methods, notation, inequalities, calculus
facts, linear functional analysis, and measure theory. Exercises are omitted
except for selected results that carry an active formalization dependency.
