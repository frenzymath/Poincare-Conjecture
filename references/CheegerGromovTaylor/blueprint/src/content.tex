\chapter*{About This Blueprint}

This project follows Jeff Cheeger, Mikhail Gromov, and Michael Taylor,
\emph{Finite propagation speed, kernel estimates for functions of the Laplace
operator, and the geometry of complete Riemannian manifolds}, published in the
\emph{Journal of Differential Geometry} 17 (1982), 15--53
\cite{CheegerGromovTaylor1982}. The source PDF is available open access from
\href{https://projecteuclid.org/journals/journal-of-differential-geometry/volume-17/issue-1/Finite-propagation-speed-kernel-estimates-for-functions-of-the-Laplace/10.4310/jdg/1214436699.pdf}{Project Euclid}.

The article relates finite propagation speed for the wave equation to kernel
estimates for functions of the Laplace operator, then derives geometric
estimates involving curvature, volume, injectivity radius, and injectivity
angle on complete Riemannian manifolds. These tools support the comparison
geometry and analytic arguments used in Ricci-flow formalization.

This blueprint is a concise, independently worded mathematical distillation,
not a verbatim copy of the article. It retains the statements, estimates,
conventions, and proof ideas needed for formalization and is intended to be
read alongside the source PDF. The \emph{dcref} tag records the corresponding
numbered item in the article.

The blueprint covers the complete article: the introduction and four sections
on finite propagation speed, kernel estimates under Ricci-curvature bounds,
bounded geometry, and volume and injectivity estimates.
