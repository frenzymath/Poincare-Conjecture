\chapter*{About This Blueprint}

This project follows John W. Morgan and Gang Tian, \emph{Ricci Flow and the
Poincar\'e Conjecture}, Clay Mathematics Monographs 3 (American Mathematical
Society and Clay Mathematics Institute, 2007). The revised prepublication
manuscript is available on
\href{https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0607607}{arXiv}; the published Clay
Mathematics Monograph is available as a
\href{https://www.claymath.org/library/monographs/cmim03.pdf}{PDF}.

Morgan and Tian give a detailed proof of the Poincar\'e conjecture following
Perelman's three Ricci-flow preprints. The book develops the required
Riemannian geometry and Ricci-flow analysis, establishes non-collapsing and
classifies ancient solutions, constructs Ricci flow with surgery, and proves
finite-time extinction for the relevant three-manifolds. Together these
arguments yield the Poincar\'e conjecture and the spherical space-form
conjecture.

The blueprint is a concise, independently worded mathematical distillation,
not a transcription of the source. It retains the statements, conventions,
cross-references, and proof ideas needed for formalization. We recommend
reading it alongside the original book, which supplies the geometric
motivation, figures, and complete arguments. The \emph{uses} annotations
record dependencies between distilled items.

The blueprint covers the introduction, all eighteen numbered chapters, and
the appendix: background Riemannian geometry, Ricci flow, comparison and
convergence results, non-collapsing, canonical neighborhoods, surgery,
finite-time extinction, and the topological conclusion.
