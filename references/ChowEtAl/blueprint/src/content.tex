\chapter*{About This Blueprint}

This project follows Bennett Chow and collaborators, \emph{The Ricci Flow:
Techniques and Applications}.  It records the geometric, analytic, and
long-time tools used in Ricci-flow singularity analysis and in the
formalization of the Poincare conjecture.

The blueprint is a concise, independently worded mathematical distillation,
not a copy of the source.  It retains definitions, statements, conventions,
and proof ideas relevant to formalization.  The \emph{dcref} metadata records
the corresponding location in the source.

The source volumes are available as publisher eBook/PDF editions:
\href{https://bookstore.ams.org/surv-135/}{Part I},
\href{https://bookstore.ams.org/surv-144/}{Part II},
\href{https://bookstore.ams.org/surv-163/}{Part III}, and
\href{https://bookstore.ams.org/surv-206/}{Part IV}.

The available audited material covers Chapters 10--36 and Appendices D--K.
Chapters 1--9 are not included: the available Part I source scan contains
front matter and appendix pages, but not the chapter text required for an
audited blueprint.  The chapter counter begins at 10 so that this gap is
visible in the rendered document.
