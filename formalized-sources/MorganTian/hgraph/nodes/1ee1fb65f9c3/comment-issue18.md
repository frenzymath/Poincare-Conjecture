`IsConjugatePointAt` is endpoint-explicit (`J 0 = 0` and `J t₁ = 0`) with a
separate nontriviality witness. Its consumers supply the geodesic hypothesis;
the predicate itself remains a Jacobi-pair condition on a curve.
